# Deploy on Render (free)

1. Go to https://render.com and sign in with GitHub.
2. New + -> Blueprint -> pick this repo -> Apply.
3. Wait ~2 minutes. Render gives you a public URL - share it with your team.

Notes
- Free plan sleeps after ~15 min idle; first open takes ~30-50 sec.
- Instagram may block cloud server IPs. If public pages show "Private, missing or blocked", that's why.
